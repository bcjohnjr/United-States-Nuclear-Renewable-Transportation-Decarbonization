# V9.19 conditional cost targets and limits

- The published V9.18 central solve is retained as a reference-cost case; resource evidence, service, lifecycle boundaries and petroleum reference costs are not changed to force a favorable comparison.
- Target technology costs are 75% of the distance from the reference parameters to inherited lower sensitivity bounds. Strong progress uses the full distance. See `us919_levers.csv` for explicit values and illustrative annual decline/three-doubling learning equivalents. These are not fitted learning curves or independently validated endpoints. The national model retains the prior reactor learning iteration.
- Target and strong non-energy annual BEV ownership differences are -$300 and -$400 per vehicle respectively. The same change is applied to the AEO2026 projected BEV stock for the matched comparison. A separate stylized common electricity-cost pass-through illustrates comparator sensitivity; neither represents a comprehensive reoptimization of AEO2026.
- Full crop collection is an imposed floor of 102.644 Mt/year; free-crop, no-crop and mapped-category restrictions are also solved. The floor is not demonstrated to be optimal or sustainably attainable by individual farms. Wet waste and methane programs retain their national evidence constraints. No credit is added for future unverified benefits.
- The 512 seeded joint draws use assumed distributions over technology cost progress, BEV ownership, resources and agricultural methane inputs; crop collection is not forced in these draws. Frequencies are conditional screening frequencies, not probabilities of real-world success.
- Annualized transition comparisons keep 2050 transport service and petroleum cases fixed; no historical demand prediction, financing cash-flow schedule, retirement schedule, hourly dispatch or capacity-constrained construction model is implied. The gated case assumes exogenous readiness, a 10 percentage-point annual adoption limit and an illustrative additional transition reserve. No actual readiness date or physical build rate is demonstrated.
- Context (not validation of precise endpoints): NREL 2024 Annual Technology Baseline, https://atb.nrel.gov/electricity/2024/technologies ; IEA EV total cost of ownership tools and analysis. The numeric assumptions are all explicit in the code and inherited reference model, not attributed as exact estimates to those sources.
