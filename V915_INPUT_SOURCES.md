# V9.15 source and assumption register

**Status:** conditional, annual incremental screening model; not a full 2050 agricultural forecast or a spatial optimisation. The V9.14.1 transport code and its previously validated release are retained without modification. No modelled programme is removed by this revision.

## Key analyst decisions (all scenario inputs visible in us915_methane.py)
- Manure national potential allocated by historical methane emissions shares as a proxy, NOT empirically observed recoverable methane by livestock class. Collection applies only to eligible, presently unrecovered feedstock. The eligibility parameter 35/60/80% is a placeholder for verified existing-project, collection, competing-use and site feasibility screening; collection 60/80/95% and biological realization 80/90/98% follow. Exclude pasture manure from actual collection unless contained and eligible.
- Organic waste divided 75% food and 25% green plant material as an analyst proxy under one NREL cap. The green fraction is eligible municipal/food-processing vegetation only, not the 166 Mt of dry farm residues already allocated to HTL. No extra HTL crop/forest biomass diverted to AD in the central case. A true allocation requires rerunning the coupled LP with a common resource constraint and county logistics; do not count the same biomass twice. Used cooking oil already reserved in renewable liquid fuel routes is not allocated to digestion.
- Gross methane = ceiling × remaining eligibility × collection × biological realization. Delivered = gross minus whole-chain fugitive leakage, flared gas and parasitic gas. Assumes flare and parasitic gas are oxidised without residual methane slip beyond the whole-chain leak parameter; process-emission allowance includes other activities (collection, upgrading, digestate emissions/N2O, energy and infrastructure proxies). No measured national process inventory. Counterfactual manure avoided fraction 35/60/80% of realised gross methane, capped by historical inventory; not a conversion factor established from farm-specific baseline management. All new leakage subtracted at GWP28. No blanket direct methane avoidance for other feedstocks.
- Programme logistics: collection at farms/food processors/municipal sites, use local covered tanks and short routes, separated clean organic wastes, sealed transport, co-digestion where permitted, covered digestate storage, nutrient return based on soil tests. Regional route distances, seasonal storage losses and plant-specific feedstock compatibility require county-level data. Costs are all-in indicative annualized plant+collection+upgrading USD/gross MWh with explicit monitoring cost; no free waste heat or carbon credits. Biomethane sale value assumed USD/delivered MWh; no central displaced fossil emission credit. External matched gas combustion sensitivity assumes 202 kg CO2/MWh LHV, not also credited to transport; no upstream fossil-gas credit. No sale and displacement double claim in transport objective.
- Monitoring: continuous fixed methane sensors, gas-flow reconciliation, periodic optical gas imaging, documented leak detection and repair, covered digestate storage, upgrading offgas control, flare performance verification and independent audits. Compare 5% leakage without monitoring vs 1% whole-chain target with explicit monitoring cost; not a guaranteed performance level.
- Digestate physical screening: assumed realized CH4 yield 0.20 t/t volatile solids, elemental N/P/K 0.04/0.008/0.02 t/t VS, 80% retention. Outputs are indicative potential, NOT incremental mineral fertilizer substitution. Zero central fertilizer revenue/abatement because manure may already return nutrients to soils. Require plant-available fraction, local crop uptake limits, contaminants, existing manure return, storage N losses, and net soil N2O before any credit. No transfer of digestate to Asparagopsis without isolated treatment and feed-grade testing. These coefficients are explicitly analyst assumptions.
- Asparagopsis target from V7: 0.43075475 Mt dry/year and 45.01626 Mt CO2e/year gross at inherited adoption rates. Further adoption, efficacy, and bioactive retention factors applied separately. Central 0.8/0.85/0.8; independent uncertainty. Assumed standalone annualized cultivation, nutrient, heat and delivery cost $5,000/t and partial lifecycle burden 10 t CO2e/t dry; no free heat, RAS nutrient, farm revenue, carbon or avoided fertilizer credits. Gross heat and N/P needs inherited from V6 (25 MWh/t, 36 kg N and 2.7 kg P/t). Need year-round feeding, active-compound stability, residue safety, animal welfare, grazing delivery, permits, and independent lifecycle verification. Older RAS nutrient-limited supply and full community economics preserved separately; no automatic transfer from already allocated transport or community heat.
- Central standalone cost net of assumed gas revenue is not a societal cost estimate; no gas revenue sensitivity is included. Cost and emissions results are incremental to a fixed transport solution, not co-optimised or empirical. Uncertainty uses 1,000 seeded triangular diagnostic draws, not predictive probabilities or a joint transport/agriculture distribution. No change to AEO baseline, 86% transport claim, or V9.14 crop uncertainty.
