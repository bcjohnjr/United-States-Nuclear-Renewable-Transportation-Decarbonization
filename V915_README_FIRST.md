# V9.15 — integrated transport and agricultural methane screening revision

**Start here. This is a research revision for author/referee review, not a cleared final submission.**

The active documents are the four `*V9.15*.docx` files. The active new data are `US915_Methane_Results.json` and the three `us915_methane_*.csv` files. `V915_INPUT_SOURCES.md` lists the sources, assumptions, limitations, and controls. The transport results are retained from V9.14.1, not rerun or reoptimised. The central case adds 33.41 Mt CO2e/year of incremental partial-boundary agricultural/waste abatement, for an indicative 1,442.83 Mt/year combined with the transport model. The continued-petroleum matched 2050 scenario is still outstanding.

## Coverage and limits

Manure (dairy, swine, poultry, beef, other), food/processing waste, green plant waste, wastewater, biomethane recovery, continuous monitoring and leak detection, digestate nutrient conservation, and year-round Asparagopsis supplementation are explicit new screening pathways. No double allocation of the 166 Mt crop residues, used cooking oil, heat or existing gas recovery is intended. The new module retains zero central credits for fertilizer displacement and external fossil gas displacement; alternate fossil displacement sensitivity is isolated. Existing Grid Harvester, offshore seaweed, aquaculture, greenhouse, drying, hydrogen, nuclear, carbon products and biomass analyses remain in the archive. A national spatial and temporal collection optimization, coupled energy dispatch, harmonised 2050 agricultural counterfactual, verified feed safety and measured emissions factors are still needed. Do not use the combined absolute abatement as a percentage of transport emissions. The new diagnostic draws are not a joint transport/agriculture probability distribution. See source register.
