# V9.17 — evidence-constrained US transport and agricultural methane screening

## Run the current release in a flat directory
Extract the **GitHub code/data ZIP** to an empty directory. Do not extract nested data archives. Python 3.13 was tested; Python 3.14 and Windows batch wrappers have not been execution-tested here. Install `python -m pip install -r requirements-v917.txt` (or use `INSTALL_V917.bat`) once. Then run `RUN_V917.bat` on Windows or `sh RUN_V917.sh` on Linux/macOS. No downloads, login, API keys, absolute paths or internet are used after dependencies are installed. The original AEO workbook archive is read directly; no nested directory is needed. Main numerical output: `US917_Evidence_Results.json`, `us917_evidence_scenarios.csv`, `us917_waste_streams.csv`, `us917_bt23_national_curves.csv`, `us917_state_historical_resources.csv`, `us917_state_crop_allocation.csv`. The final validation tests exact legacy regression, crop-group caps, mass and cost balances and 72 original image hashes. New figure S33 is generated by `us917_figures.py`; earlier figures are never regenerated by the new runner.

The runner reruns the legacy methane diagnostic and central/no-crop transport and gas routing, then three new evidence-constrained national scenarios with five references. The old 1,000-draw transport uncertainty results are retained; they are not newly calibrated V9.17 draws. Historical scripts and outputs are retained for continuity; previous editorial masters/archives are not in the GitHub-only ZIP. Historical full V9.14 validation requiring original document masters can be run from the previous complete research archive. Do not upload old submissions as current figures. The separate editorial archive has active V9.17 DOCX and 38 TIFFs, numbered to match the manuscript.

## Evidence scope
- Public BT23 national workbook: 3 mature-market scenarios × 9 offered prices × 5 crop resources. Short ton × 0.90718474 = metric tonne. Workbook is a modelled national supply scenario (2041 mature-market), not observed supply or the unavailable county-level price curve. Price points $60/$90/$120 per dry short ton floor mapped delivered-feedstock price after conversion, with explicit additional $25/$15/$10 per metric tonne delivery assumption; soil/nutrient cost remains separate. Price-year harmonisation remains approximate. Candidate classes outside these groups retain analyst assumptions. Crop national cap = min(166 Mt, historical crop residue total × 0.65/0.80/1.0); forest-only cap = min(150 Mt, historical forest total × the same fraction). Neither is a verified sustainable collection limit. State allocations are proportional diagnostics, not a spatial optimization.
- USDA county layer: 3,221 records; 3,138 numeric including DC. National model aggregates 50 states (3,137 numeric); five Alaska and 78 Puerto Rico records are missing, never imputed. County historic values are dry metric tonnes or metric tonnes CH4 according to original metadata. Historic manure emissions are a separate upper bound, not additional emissions added to EPA's inventory.
- NWWI point inventory (2012–2016 vintages, published 2020): 56,111 rows; historical dry solids, not current unused supplies. Intersect original methane ceilings with 0.10/0.15/0.20 t CH4 per t dry solids. Potentially overlapping food-processing and other crop solids are each bounded at a quarter of the NWWI food-waste mass; selected mass reserved before biogas. Municipal green waste excludes field residues; poultry/other manure and green waste retain legacy analyst ceilings, not NWWI evidence. No additional FOG fuel, fertilizer credit, food revenue or free heat assumed. Eligibility assumptions proxy remaining supply after existing recovery, not calibrated from facility counts. Existing AgSTAR and CWNS counts and 2022 cattle/atlas evidence are audit diagnostics. Cattle supplement efficacy, safety, adoption, external nutrient and heat costs remain assumed. Internal-first gas substitution uses each delivered unit only once; no carbon-price revenue. The full model is sequential, not simultaneous hourly/spatial planning.
- Source URLs in V6 report, V4 manifest and prepared source integrity records. The exact ORNL county price export is still pending authorization. Historical Parquet, full food waste GIS, complete source PDFs not needed for this runner are retained in the user's existing evidence downloads; no need to publish them for numerical reproduction. V6 archive includes all 50 fertilizer PDFs and raw county data. No false claim that these PDFs or historical Parquet rows calibrate this release's NPK coefficients or delivered prices.
- Prior V9.16 result is an assumption-based sensitivity; V9.17 central is the more constrained public-data scenario. Neither full lifecycle harmonisation nor a national agricultural counterfactual is achieved. The two price cases are inherited price scenarios, not future forecasts. No claim of national-sector or worldwide percentage including agriculture; combined absolute CO2e savings only.
